## Minimal decision experiments to settle disputes without fake ROI

1. **R-54 test:** For the same client flow, remove `2.1.3` external channel, then remove `3.1.9` mandate, then remove verified `6.1.6` evidence. Record whether any **lawful** P1/P2 operation remains. Compare functionality, not estimated revenue.
2. **A2 vs D1 test:** Comparable guarantee customers; arm A: existing guarantee via current sales, arm B: same guarantee + agent quote channel, arm C: enhanced guarantee + agent channel. Evaluate risk-adjusted contribution after customer acquisition and pricing compression.
3. **A1 vs standard SCF test:** Same legal obligor risk / anchor and same claim quality; compare accepted deep-tier funding, losses, verification cost and total funding time. Avoid cherry-picking best suppliers.
4. **A3 standalone test:** Same contract through ordinary escrow S0 vs premium fact-based S1 vs optional S2. Compare contractual legality, false release, dispute burden, customer costs and incremental bank margin.

**Decision rule:** If no positive incremental performance is proven on the appropriate baseline, retain the FBS as necessary operational ability but **remove unsupported product premium**. If official FBS scope cannot be established, mark `UNMAPPED`, do not invent a code.
